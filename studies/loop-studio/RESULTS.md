## What the actual visuals show

Ordinary combines organic nested Pulse outlines, flat braided Weave strokes and smooth interlocking Orbit toruses, satellites, a reflective center and a strong ground shadow. Motion uses 48 stable capsule objects with radial Pulse, braided Weave and interlocking Orbit formations, reflected studio light, contrasting surfaces and visible occlusion. These are substantive different compositions. Motion keeps its complete specimen beside the desktop editor; ordinary's taller hero can extend above the viewport while its desktop controls are in view. Mobile ordinary adds a compact specimen alongside editing controls. These observations identify design choices, not an overall quality score.

Actual decoded reversal frames show ordinary crossfading between compositions and Motion retargeting its capsule formation. Both retain the selected final Orbit state. The moving shapes remain visible during the reviewed transitions; intermediate overlaps and crossings occur. Full footage and native state observations are preserved separately. Approximate video alignment and 25fps VP8 recordings cannot establish 60fps smoothness. The PNG pause checks provide independent evidence of a frozen selected pose, rather than treating codec noise as motion.

SVG is a static vector snapshot, not a video or a physically lit 3D scene. Only the assisted prompt added `$motion` and the installed five-skill/MCP intervention. This is one generation pair, not a causal attribution or a general agent benchmark. Model/effort are configured CLI settings; provider-wire identity is unavailable.
