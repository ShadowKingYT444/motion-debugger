THE SATURDAY REPAIR CAFÉ
An illustrative plan from the Anthropic Ants design concept.
20 visitors · 3 work areas · $120 budget

SCOPE
Host a two-hour session, 10 am–noon, in an accessible community room. Invite clothing, small household items, and bicycles. Ask about access needs at registration.

PREPARE
Set up a welcome desk and three marked work areas. Recruit an experienced volunteer for each plus a coordinator. Provide tools, thread, patches, bike supplies, and safety instructions. Leave electrical repairs to qualified people.

INVITE
Share a flyer one week ahead with the address, access information, what to bring, and a sign-up contact. Limit attendance to twenty visitors and ask them to describe their item.

RUN THE MORNING
9:30 — Volunteer setup and tool check.
10:00 — Welcome, triage, and assign work areas.
10:15 — Repair together; explain each step.
11:45 — Share lessons and clean up.
12:00 — Close; collect notes for next time.

BUDGET
$50 supplies; $30 refreshments; $20 printing; $20 contingency.
Borrow tools. Confirm the room is free before inviting people.

NEXT ACTION
Confirm the room and three volunteer leads. Then share the invitation.

This is a scripted local demonstration, not an announced Anthropic product or professional repair advice.
