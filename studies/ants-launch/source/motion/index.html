ANTHROPIC ANTS / FIELD NOTES

ABOUT THE CONCEPT

Small by design.
Shared by nature.

This is an independent interface exploration of coordinated, specialized agents. Anthropic Ants is not an announced product and this page is not affiliated with Anthropic.

The example is a scripted local demonstration. No AI model is called, no personal data is collected, and no task is sent anywhere.

How to explore

Launch the example to see four stages. Pause freezes the task and colony together; Cancel returns the workers to their starting positions. Replay runs the same example again. Scroll toward the task to bring the workshop closer.

Prefer less motion? Your system preference removes camera travel and decorative movement. The complete task and plan remain available.

FINISHED ARTIFACT / ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE

The Saturday
repair café.

20 visitors · 3 work areas · $120 budget

01 / Set the scope

Host a two-hour session, 10 am–noon, in an accessible community room. Invite neighbors to bring clothing, small household items, and bicycles. Ask about access needs at registration.

02 / Prepare the room

Set up a welcome desk and three clearly marked work areas. Recruit one experienced volunteer per area plus a coordinator. Provide tools, thread, patches, basic bike supplies, and safety instructions. Leave electrical repairs to qualified people.

03 / Invite the neighborhood

Share a simple flyer one week ahead: what to bring, the address, access information, and a sign-up contact. Limit the first session to twenty visitors. Ask attendees to describe their item so volunteers can prepare.

04 / Run the morning

  • 9:30 — Volunteers set up and check tools.
  • 10:00 — Welcome, triage, and assign work areas.
  • 10:15 — Repair together; explain each step.
  • 11:45 — Share what worked and clean up.
  • 12:00 — Close; collect notes for the next café.

05 / Keep the budget simple

$50 supplies · $30 refreshments · $20 printing · $20 contingency. Borrow tools and confirm the room is free before inviting attendees.

Next action: confirm the room and three volunteer leads. Then share the invitation.
