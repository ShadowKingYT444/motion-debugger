THE IDEA BEHIND ANTS

Less solo effort.
More shared purpose.

Good teamwork makes room for different kinds of attention. Ants imagines small helpers with clear roles, a common goal, and a deliberate way to pass work along.

01 / DIVIDE WITH INTENTION

Give every ant
a useful role.

A shared brief becomes a few bounded assignments. Everyone knows what they are carrying.

02 / MAKE THE WORK VISIBLE

Many paths.
One direction.

Independent work meets at a shared handoff. Progress is visible, and you can pause or stop the colony.

03 / FINISH SOMETHING

The artifact
is the point.

The colony comes together around something you can use. Here, that means a simple event plan.

Small is a starting point.
