# MONO 01 initial build

Independent product concept implementation for the MONO brief.

- Stack: Vite 7.3.6, vanilla JavaScript, Three.js 0.180.0.
- Product presentation: two low-detail procedural headphone models use shared material and view state. Scene controls, pointer orbit, scroll-linked turning, and section reveals are local.
- Commerce: three finish options update the 3D product, description, concept price, and cart. Quantity edits, removal, undo, and the checkout preview work in local state only.
- Scope: the price and acoustic figures are illustrative design-study values. There is no checkout service or remote API.
- Motion: section entry, product rotation, finish change, sticky purchase bar, cart drawer, cart line entry, undo toast, and response-curve reveal use local CSS/Three.js motion. Reduced-motion preferences are supported.
- Verification: pending live browser review at desktop and narrow widths.
