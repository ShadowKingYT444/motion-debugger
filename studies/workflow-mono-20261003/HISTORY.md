The fan recordings were only baseline diagnostics. Their earlier weak timing, broken keyboard reopening and later handwritten functional repair are not reused as a new successful comparison. This report preserves incomplete work rather than presenting it as a completed or favorable trial.

Final consumer verification first failed the unchanged sampling gate; all three raw recordings are retained in `evidence/final-consumer-attempt-1`. One sequential verification after the unit suite completed passed and is retained in `evidence/final-consumer-attempt-2`. Neither changes the frozen coding arms or establishes a quality advantage.
