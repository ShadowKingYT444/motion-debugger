01 — Studio

A tree needs eighty years to become a beam. We think a building should be worth the wait. So we draw slowly, choose timber we can trace to a single forest, and detail every joint so it can be opened, mended and loved for another century.

I

Traceable

Every beam is logged back to a named stand of forest. Clients receive the felling map along with the drawings.

II

Repairable

Joinery over glue, bolts over resin. A panel, a board or a post can be lifted out and replaced in an afternoon.

III

Civic

A building should give more to the street than it takes: shelter, shade, a bench, a reason to stay a minute longer.

03 — Process

From a hillside
to a house.

  1. 01

    Site

    We spend the first month on the land, not at the desk. Sun angles, snow load, the way water leaves the slope.

  2. 02

    Frame

    Glulam posts and beams are cut in the workshop to a millimetre, numbered, then raised in days rather than months.

  3. 03

    Envelope

    Walls close in vertical larch boards, left to silver or oiled red. The detail is made to be opened and mended.

  4. 04

    Light

    Windows are set last and by hand, against the sun path we drew on day one. Then the keys change hands.
