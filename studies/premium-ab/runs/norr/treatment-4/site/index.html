01 Studio philosophy

We believe a building should be a slow crop. Grown in the forest for a century, shaped in the workshop for a season, and worn into beauty by the people who use it for generations after.

  1. i.

    Material honesty

    Timber carries load, warms the hand and shows its age. We never paint over what the tree has already said, and we never hide a joint that is doing real work.

  2. ii.

    Daylight is a material

    A northern winter is a design brief, not an obstacle. Every opening is placed to catch the longest hour of low sun and to keep it inside the room.

  3. iii.

    Built to be repaired

    Dry joints, bolted frames, drawings that outlive the practice. A building that can be mended is a building that is kept.

03 Process — raising a building

01

Read the site

We walk the land for a full season before drawing a line: sun angles, snow load, wind, and the trees that should stay.

02

Raise the frame

Glulam portal frames, cut by CNC and assembled dry on site in days. Every joint can be unbolted — and repaired.

03

Wrap in a skin

Locally milled spruce and pine, left untreated to silver. Breathable walls, no plastic membranes, no mystery layers.

04

Let in the light

Windows go in last and are set for the longest winter hour. We hand the building over when the first fire is lit.
