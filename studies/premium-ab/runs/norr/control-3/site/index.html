01 — Studio philosophy

Founded 2008

We build with the one material that removes carbon as it grows. Every beam in an Atelier Norr building was once a tree on a slow hillside, and we design so it can be read as one: grain, joint, load and light, all left honest.

i.

Slow material

Spruce and larch from certified forests within 200 km, felled in winter and dried for a year before a single joint is cut.

ii.

Public warmth

A civic building should feel like a room the whole town owns. Timber makes that easy to say and very hard to fake.

iii.

Honest joinery

No hidden steel where a joint can do the work. What holds the roof up is what you see when you look up.

03 — Process

From hillside to handover

Five seasons of care.

01Listen to the site

  1. 01

    Listen to the site

    We walk the plot through a full year if the client allows it. Sun arcs, snow loads, wind corridors, the path a neighbour already takes. The first drawing is just a diagram of what the land asks for.

  2. 02

    Choose the trees

    Before there is a plan, there is a timber budget. We visit the sawmill and mark the stands, so structure, grade and the length of every beam follow the forest instead of the other way round.

  3. 03

    Draw & model

    Every joint is modelled before it is cut: parametric frames, full-scale mock-ups, and a physical model by hand. Fire, acoustics and carbon are solved in the drawing, not on site.

  4. 04

    Prefabricate

    Walls, floors and frames are milled and assembled indoors, to the millimetre, in dry winter workshops. A building arrives as a kit of numbered parts and goes up in weeks, not seasons.

  5. 05

    Raise & tend

    We stay for the raising, and return after the first winter to oil, adjust and listen. Timber moves; a good building is one that was designed to age in company.
