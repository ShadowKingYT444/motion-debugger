01  —  The object

Turned, not assembled.

One aluminium cap, one seamless wool sleeve, one cast-mineral foot. Nothing to look at but the light, and nothing that looks like a speaker.

Twelve drivers, one ring.

Textile-dome drivers fire outward in a perfect circle, phase-locked to the microsecond, so sound arrives from the room rather than from a box.

Dressed in knit and metal.

Danish wool stretched over a 3D-knit frame. It breathes sound, sheds dust, and lets the anodised cap catch the evening.

A small brain that listens.

Four microphones measure your room in forty seconds, then step out of the way. A hardware switch cuts them off entirely.

02  —  Sound technology

Ring Array. The room, played back.

Twelve drivers radiate outward in a circle instead of forward in a line. Every wall becomes a second source, and the Ring Array computes how to use it. Move the listener on the plan and watch the first reflections arrive.
