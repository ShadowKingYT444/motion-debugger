01 — The object

One cylinder, taken apart.

01 / FORM

An unbroken cylinder

190 millimetres across, 250 tall, 3.4 kilograms. Heavy enough to settle into a shelf, quiet enough to be left on all day. There is no front, so there is no wrong way to place it.

Ø 190 mm250 mm3.4 kg
02 / CROWN

The halo itself

A 3 mm ring of anodised aluminium holds the light and the touch. Glide a finger around it for volume, tap for play, hold to pair. Nothing to download, nothing to sign in to.

03 / CORE

Nine drivers, nine amplifiers

Eight silk-dome tweeters circle the crown. One long-throw woofer fires down into the oak. Two passive radiators breathe from the sides. Each driver gets its own channel of amplification.

04 / BASE

Oak, cut in Jutland

A solid oak foot lifts the cylinder 6 mm off the surface, so the woofer can breathe and the shelf stays out of the conversation. Oiled by hand. It will darken, and look better for it.

02 — Sound technology

Sound that doesn’t have a front.

Conventional speakers beam sound toward one seat. Halo’s Ring Array launches it in every direction at once, then listens to your room for twelve seconds and shapes the rest.

Drag the speaker anywhere

of the room within ±3 dB of the median level.

QuietLoud

Illustrative acoustic model. Not a measurement.

Ring Array

Eight tweeters, one wavefront

Eight 19 mm silk-dome drivers sit on a 120 mm circle, phase-matched so neighbours sum into one smooth wave instead of a bouquet of beams.

Downfire woofer

Bass you feel in the room

A 133 mm long-throw driver radiates into the gap above the oak and spreads across the floor — low end that arrives from everywhere, not from one corner.

Room tune

Twelve seconds, then silence

Six MEMS microphones listen to a single sweep, model the room, and are then powered down. They are switched off in hardware unless you ask for a retune.

Wide, flat, quietly deep.

From 38 Hz to 22 kHz, within three decibels. The design target for the on-axis response in an anechoic chamber.

03 — Materials

Made to be touched, not hidden.

Three materials, chosen because they age well and sound honest. Nothing is plastic-coated to look like something else.

Wool knit

I.

Knitted in Denmark from recycled wool-blend yarn. Seamless, tension-fitted and open enough to be invisible to the drivers. The sleeve unzips, so a new colour is a gift, not a new speaker.

Finish

Solid oak

II.

FSC-certified oak, quarter-sawn for a quiet grain and finished with hard-wax oil. It will darken and pick up marks, and look better each year it stays on the shelf.

Origin
Jutland, Denmark
Finish

Brushed aluminium

III.

One ring of anodised aluminium, brushed against the grain by hand and sealed. Cool to the touch, 3 mm thick, and the only part of Halo that glows.

04 — Room-filling

It learns the room. Then disappears into it.

Halo is built for the way homes are actually used: set on a shelf, moved to the kitchen, paired with a twin, left alone for a week.

Room tuning

Hears the room once.

A twelve-second sweep maps reflections, corners and furniture. Halo then adjusts each driver to cancel the boom and keep the air.
