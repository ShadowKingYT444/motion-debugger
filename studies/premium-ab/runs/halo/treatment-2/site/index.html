Why Halo

We did not set out to make a louder speaker. We set out to make a room sound the way the recording felt: even, close and unhurried, from one quiet object you forget is there.

The object

  1. Close it, and it disappears.

    A 196 mm cylinder with no visible seam, button or grille. Scroll, and we take it apart.

  2. A line of light.

    One 0.6 mm slot under the aluminium cap is the only interface. Touch the cap to play; the light follows your hand.

  3. Seven drivers, one wavefront.

    Six full-range drivers form a ring that fires outward in phase, over a chamber tuned to the seventh.

  4. Wool that lets sound through.

    A seamless knit of wool and recycled polyester, acoustically transparent and quiet under a hand.

  5. Floating on light.

    A recessed foot lifts the body off the surface, so the bass never couples to your shelf.

Sound technology

Seven drivers. One wavefront.

7drivers

Ring array

Six small full-range drivers sit in a circle and fire in phase, so the highs arrive together instead of beaming at one seat.

360°dispersion

Even in every direction

A waveguide cast into the cap turns the ring outward. Walk around the room and the balance stays where it was.

It listens to your room

Built-in microphones map the nearest walls and shelves, then ease the response around them. Move it, and it learns again.

Materials

Four materials. Nothing hidden.

Every surface you touch was chosen for how it sounds first, and how it ages second. Keep scrolling.

Scroll →

01

Spun aluminium

The cap is spun from a single disc of recycled aluminium, then turned and bead-blasted. It doubles as the acoustic waveguide.

02

Wool-blend knit

Knitted in one seamless tube in Jutland, with a thread count tuned to pass 21 kHz and stop dust at the door. Removable for washing.

03

Diffused light

A single warm LED, spread by a machined lip into an even line. It dims with the room and never flickers with the beat.

04

Oiled oak plinth

An optional solid-oak ring for the foot, from managed Danish forests. Each one is oiled by hand and develops its own tone.
