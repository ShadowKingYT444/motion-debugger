Anatomy

Ten drivers, one quiet object.

Keep scrolling to take Halo apart. Every layer is there for the sound, and nothing else.

01  /  Touch ring

A ring you can turn.

A single band of light caps the speaker. Trace it with a fingertip to set the volume; it brightens as the level rises and fades when you let go.

02  /  Waveguide

Six tweeters, facing out.

Silk domes sit sixty degrees apart around the rim, each behind its own waveguide. Highs reach the far wall with the same clarity as the near one.

03  /  Knit sleeve

Wool you can hear through.

A seamless wool-blend knit, acoustically transparent by design. It zips off in seconds when you want a different colour, or a wash.

04  /  Woofer

Bass that goes sideways.

A 6.5 inch long-throw woofer fires down into a conical reflector, spreading low frequencies evenly in every direction instead of at one wall.

05  /  Base

Weight is a feature.

A milled aluminium base on a cork ring turns vibration into stillness. The music stays in the air, not in the shelf it sits on.

Assembled

Five parts. One object.

Each Halo is assembled, tuned and listened to by hand in Copenhagen before it leaves the workshop.

Our idea

We did not build a speaker to be looked at. We built it to disappear, and to leave your room sounding like itself, only larger.

Halo Array

One ring of drivers. Every seat is the good seat.

Most speakers throw sound forward and hope you are standing there. Halo radiates in a full circle, so the level a few steps to the left is the level where you are. Drag the speaker around the room and watch the five listeners.

Materials

01

Anodised aluminium

One solid 6063 billet for the cap, another for the base. Bead-blasted, then anodised in small batches so the edge catches light and never chips.

Cap and base · 6063 alloy

02

Wool-blend knit

A single seamless tube knitted to size. Open enough for sound to pass untouched, dense enough to hide everything behind it.

Sleeve · 70% wool, 30% recycled polyester

03

Natural cork

A damping ring under the base. Cork is light, warm and exceptionally good at ignoring vibration, which is exactly the job.

Isolation ring · 12 mm

04

Silk-dome tweeters

Six 19 mm domes, matched in pairs on the bench. Silk keeps the top end smooth where metal gets bright.

Ring · 19 mm × 6

05

Diffused light

The halo is a light guide etched in the cap itself: no pixels, no icons, just one warm line that follows your hand.
