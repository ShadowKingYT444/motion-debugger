The object

Four parts. Nothing to hide.

  1. 01

    One knitted sleeve

    A seamless tube of recycled wool blend, knitted to an acoustic weave that is 94% open. It has no grille, no seam and no logo.

  2. 02

    Six drivers in a ring

    Six 1-inch full-range drivers face outward at 60° intervals. A 5-inch woofer fires down into the plinth gap and uses the table as its horn.

  3. 03

    The halo

    A machined aluminium waveguide spreads the ring evenly. Its light is the only interface: tap to play, turn to set the level.

  4. 04

    Walnut, floating on light

    A solid walnut plinth isolates the cabinet from your furniture. The gap is lit from below, so the speaker seems to hover.

Sound technology

It does not point. It surrounds.

Corners swell the bass. Shelves smear the middle. Halo listens to where it has been put, then corrects itself so the music stays the same.

Floor plan · 5 × 3.5 m living roomBoundary gain +6 dB

Response at the sofa20 Hz – 20 kHz
Placed as isHalo corrected
+7 dBBass swells in a corner. Halo trims 80 Hz and 160 Hz before they reach you.
360°

Even in every direction

A conical waveguide above the ring keeps output within ±1.5 dB around the full circle, so there is no front and no best seat.

7

Channels, one phase

Seven amplifier channels with FIR phase correction make the downfiring woofer and the ring arrive at your ear together.

20s

To learn your room

A 20-second sweep, heard by four built-in microphones, builds a correction for where Halo sits. Move it and it asks to listen again.

Materials

Chosen to be touched.

Three materials, each doing a job. Choose a finish and watch the object change; all four use the same wool, wood and metal, dyed or oiled differently.

Fjord · blue-grey wool, walnut

Finishes are rendered here as drawings. Real colours vary a little with the dye lot, which is the point of wool.

Shell

Knitted wool

Recycled wool and polyester, knitted in one piece on a circular machine in Jutland. Acoustically transparent, and it softens with a brush, not a cloth.

Plinth

Solid timber

FSC-certified walnut or oak, turned from a single block and finished with hard-wax oil. Every plinth has its own grain, so no two look alike.

Halo

Machined aluminium

The waveguide is turned from a solid billet, then bead-blasted and anodised. It stays cool to the touch, and carries the light that tells you what it is doing.
