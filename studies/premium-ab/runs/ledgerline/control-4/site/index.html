01 Product

One API for the whole money lifecycle.

Stop stitching together a payments processor, a spreadsheet and a nightly cron job. Ledgerline gives your engineers a single, programmable source of truth for every cent.

01 / LEDGERS

Double-entry, by construction.

Every transfer is a balanced set of entries. Wallets, escrow, fees and FX are just accounts in a tree you define in code, and the books close themselves.

  • Immutable, append-only journal
  • Real-time balances with holds and limits
  • Multi-currency accounts with FX legs
02 / PAYMENTS

Every rail, one call.

ACH, RTP, FedNow, SEPA Instant, wires and cards behind a single endpoint. Ledgerline routes for speed and cost, and posts the result to your ledger atomically.

  • Smart routing with automatic failover
  • Idempotent by default, retry-safe everywhere
  • Webhooks with signed, replayable events
03 / RECONCILIATION

Close the books continuously.

Bank statements and processor files are matched against your ledger as they land. Breaks surface with a suggested fix, not a week-end spreadsheet hunt.

  • Auto-match on reference, amount and timing
  • Exception queue with one-click resolution
  • Audit-ready exports for finance
