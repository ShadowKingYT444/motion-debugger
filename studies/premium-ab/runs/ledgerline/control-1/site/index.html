    Life of a payment

    From request to settled in four steps.

    1. 01Authorize

      Your request is validated, idempotency-keyed and routed to the best rail in under 10 ms.

    2. 02Hold

      Funds are reserved against the source account and a pending entry is written, so nothing can be spent twice.

    3. 03Post

      An atomic journal moves value between accounts. If debits and credits do not match, it does not commit.

    4. 04Settle

      The rail confirms, entries finalize and a signed payment.settled event reaches your service.
