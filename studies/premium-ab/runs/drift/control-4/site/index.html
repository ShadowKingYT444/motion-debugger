How it works

Three quiet steps,
every single night.

  1. Drift hears your room and reads your phone or watch: breathing, movement, noise and temperature. No cameras. Audio is analysed on-device and never stored.

  2. As you drift from light to deep sleep, the AI re-mixes your soundscape in real time: softer highs, slower swells, quieter layers when you settle.

  3. Within your wake window, Drift finds the lightest moment and brings in dawn light and gentle sound, so mornings stop feeling like a cliff.

The science

Your night is not one thing.
It is a rhythm.

Sleep moves through repeating cycles of light, deep and REM sleep, roughly every 90 minutes. What helps in the first cycle can get in the way in the fourth. Drift is built to follow that rhythm instead of playing one track all night.

0

Cycles, not hours

Sleep arrives in cycles of about ninety minutes. Waking mid-cycle is what leaves you groggy, so Drift targets the lightest point inside your window.

0

Deep early, REM late

Deep sleep dominates the first half of the night, REM the second. Soundscapes ease off through REM so dreams are not interrupted by loud layers.

0

Wind-down that learns

Slow breathing and a steady, masked sound floor help the body downshift. Drift tests routines on you and keeps only what shortens your own time to sleep.
