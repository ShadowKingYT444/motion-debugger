How it works

One night, in order.

  1. 21:30Wind down
  2. 23:10Drift off

It reads your day, then lowers the lights.

Caffeine cut-off, steps, how late you went to bed this week. Drift builds tonight's wind-down from that, and starts with a warm drone and a slow, even breath.

The breath slows. The sound thins with you.

Pacing eases from twelve breaths a minute toward six. As your breathing steadies and movement drops, the rain recedes and a low noise floor takes over.

At 3 a.m. it holds the line.

A bin lorry, a door, a partner turning over. Drift lifts a quiet masking floor just ahead of the noise, so a spike becomes a blur instead of a wake-up.

Morning arrives inside your window.

Within the half hour you chose, Drift waits for lighter sleep, then brightens birdsong and swell until you surface, not until a siren drags you out.

The science

Built on what sleep research actually supports.

No miracle frequencies. Drift leans on three well-understood levers (breath, sound and light) and tunes them to you, night by night.

Drift is a wellness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders. If you're struggling, talk to a clinician.

01 · Breath

Slow, even breathing quiets the body.

Breathing around six times a minute is linked to higher heart-rate variability and a calmer, more rested state. Drift paces you there with a sound you can follow with your eyes shut.

02 · Sound

Steady sound hides sudden sound.

It's the change, not the loudness, that tends to jolt a sleeper. A smooth, low-level floor narrows the gap between quiet and a spike, so brief noises fall below notice.

03 · Rhythm

Evenings cool and dim. Mornings warm and brighten.

Your body temperature falls into sleep and light sets your clock. Drift times the wind-down and your wake window to that rhythm, so the alarm meets you on the way up.
