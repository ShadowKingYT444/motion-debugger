How it works

Four quiet steps, every night.

No dials to babysit. Drift reads the signals your evening gives off and does the adjusting for you.

  1. 01

    Listen

    From the moment you open the app, Drift tunes in: your phone's light sensor, room noise, wearable heart rate and the time since your last coffee log. A calm picture of your evening takes shape.

  2. 02

    Adapt

    A generative engine blends four sound layers and rebalances them second by second. Restless? Brown noise rises. Settling? The mix thins to near silence, never a hard stop.

  3. 03

    Guide

    Your routine reshapes itself. Breathing pace slows with your pulse, lights dim on schedule, and a ten-minute body scan appears only on the nights you need it.

  4. 04

    Wake

    In your chosen window, Drift looks for lighter sleep and brings in warm sound and sunrise light. Mornings stop feeling like an ambush.

The science

Built on how sleep actually works.

Drift pairs well-established sleep research with an adaptive model, so the app does less guessing and more listening. It’s a wellness tool, not a medical device.

Circadian alignment

Evening light and timing shift your internal clock. Drift schedules light dimming and wind-down around your own chronotype, not a one-size bedtime.

Steady, masking sound

Consistent broadband noise can soften sudden sounds that pull you out of sleep. Drift shapes it to your room instead of looping a track.

Slow, paced breathing

Breathing near six breaths a minute is linked with calmer heart-rate variability. Drift paces your breath to your pulse and eases it down.

Sleep-stage aware wake

Waking from lighter sleep often feels easier than from deep sleep. Drift searches your window for it, and never moves your alarm past your limit.
