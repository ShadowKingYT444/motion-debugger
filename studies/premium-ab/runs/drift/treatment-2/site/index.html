The idea

Most sleep sounds are a loop that never notices you. Your night is not a loop. It has a slow descent, a long middle and a gentle edge, and Drift follows all three.

How it works

One night, four hands-offs.

  1. 10:30 PM

    Wind down

    Drift hears the day end: screen off, steps quiet, breathing slows. Rain opens soft and wide, then narrows.

  2. 11:10 PM

    Settle

    When sleep onset is detected, the mix steps down by about a third so nothing holds you in light sleep.

  3. 3:00 AM

    Hold

    In deep sleep a low hum carries you. If you stir, Drift answers with the gentlest layer, never an alarm.

  4. 6:45 AM

    Wake

    Inside your window, at your lightest point, birds fade in and the screen warms. No jolt.

The science

Built on how sleep is actually shaped.

A night is not eight flat hours. It runs in cycles of roughly ninety minutes, each moving through lighter, deeper and dreaming sleep. Drift reads where you are in that arc and chooses sound to match.

White noise

Equal energy at every frequency. Bright and hissy, it masks sudden sounds well but can feel sharp for long listens.

Drift uses it for: short masking when a street is loud

Informed by published work including Zhou et al., 2012 (J. Theor. Biol.) on pink noise and sleep stability, and Papalambros et al., 2017 (Front. Hum. Neurosci.) on acoustic stimulation and slow-wave activity in older adults. Results vary by person. Drift is a wellness product and does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders.
