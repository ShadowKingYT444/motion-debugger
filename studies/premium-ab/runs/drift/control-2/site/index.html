How it works

Three quiet steps. One better night.

No dials, no schedules to babysit. Drift learns how you sleep, then adjusts itself from the first wind-down to the last moment of morning.

  1. 01 — Listen

    It learns the shape of your night.

    Drift reads quiet signals — heart rate from a watch or ring if you have one, movement and ambient sound from your phone — and builds a private model of how you fall asleep, how you stir, and what helps.

    • Optional wearable sync, never required
    • Room noise, light and temperature context
    • Gets sharper every night you use it
  2. 02 — Adapt

    Sound that responds, not loops.

    Layers of rain, wind and slow harmonic pads are mixed live. As you settle, they soften. If you stir at 1 AM, Drift lifts a layer to cover it. Your night is never the same, so your soundscape shouldn’t be either.

    • Wind-down routines paced to your breath
    • Masks the sounds that wake you, quietly
    • Fades to silence once you’re deeply asleep
  3. 03 — Wake

    Morning, at the right moment.

    Choose a window rather than a minute. Drift watches for the lightest part of your final sleep cycle and brings in a slow sunrise of light and sound, so you surface instead of being yanked out of sleep.

    • Smart alarm window up to 45 minutes
    • Sunrise light on your phone, melody that swells
    • A one-tap morning check-in to teach Drift more

The science

Built around your sleep cycles, not a clock.

A night isn’t one long stretch. It’s a rhythm of lighter and deeper phases that repeat roughly every ninety minutes. Drift is designed around that rhythm, drawing on well-established sleep research.
