How it works

A coach that stays up so you don't have to.

  1. 01

    Tell Drift about your nights

    A two-minute check-in covers your schedule, your room and what keeps you awake. Drift builds a starting routine around you, not an average sleeper.

  2. 02

    Using your phone's microphone and motion sensing, plus your wearable if you have one, Drift reads breathing, restlessness and room noise, and retunes the soundscape as you drift off.

    Instead of one blunt alarm, Drift looks for your lightest sleep inside the window you choose, and raises sunrise light and gentle chimes from there.

The science

Your night is a rhythm, not a switch.

Sleep moves through repeating cycles of light, deep and REM sleep. Drift is designed around that structure, and adjusts to your version of it.

Illustrative hypnogram. Real nights vary.

90min

Cycles of roughly ninety minutes

Most adults move through light, deep and REM sleep several times a night. Drift aims your wake window at a light stage inside each cycle, so the alarm meets less resistance.

½first half

Deep sleep arrives early

Deep sleep clusters in the first half of the night, while REM lengthens toward morning. That is why Drift gets calmer and steadier early, and holds back disturbances you do not need.

3signals

Sound that responds, not just plays

Steady, low-level sound can mask sudden noises that cause micro-arousals. Drift reads breathing, movement and room noise, and shapes sound to match, rather than looping a track all night.

Drift is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders. If you suspect one, talk to a clinician.

Questions

Good to know.

Does Drift record me while I sleep?

No. Audio is analysed on your phone to detect breathing and noise levels, then discarded. Only simple numbers, such as decibel levels and breathing rate, are kept.

Do I need a wearable?

No. Drift works with just your phone on the nightstand. If you have a compatible wearable, it can add heart-rate data for finer adaptation.

Will it drain my battery?

Overnight sensing is lightweight. Keep your phone plugged in or near 50% at bedtime and you'll wake with plenty left.

Is Drift a medical device?

No. Drift is a wellness app. It can't diagnose or treat sleep disorders, so please speak with a clinician if you're worried about your sleep.
