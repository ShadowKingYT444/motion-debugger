Why Drift

Most sleep apps play the same sound all night. Drift listens, so the night can change, and the sound changes with it, quietly, one small adjustment at a time.

How it works

One night, four adjustments.

  1. Before bed

    Listen

    Drift reads your evening — bedtime habits, room noise and, if you connect a watch, heart rate. It stays on your phone.

  2. Falling asleep

    Adapt

    Rain thins, low tide takes over, and the mix follows your breathing until you are gone.

  3. Through the night

    Hold

    Stir at 3 a.m. and Drift answers with a softer layer. Never a jolt of sound or light.

  4. Morning

    Wake

    Inside your wake window Drift finds your lightest moment and brings the dawn up slowly.

The science

Built on how sleep actually works.

Drift doesn't invent a new theory of sleep. It applies four well-understood ideas, and shows every adjustment in your morning note, so nothing is a black box.

Drift is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders. If poor sleep lasts for weeks, talk to a clinician.

01

Sleep moves in cycles

Across a night you pass through light, deep and REM sleep in loops of roughly ninety minutes. Drift plans around that rhythm instead of treating the night as one flat block.

02

Steady sound shelters sleep

A consistent, low-level soundscape can soften sudden noises that would otherwise nudge you awake. Drift keeps a steady bed of sound and lets it recede as you drift deeper.

03

Waking lightly beats waking hard

Surfacing from light sleep tends to feel gentler than being pulled out of deep sleep. Your wake window gives Drift room to find that moment before your alarm time.

04

Slow breath settles the body

Slow, paced breathing is a well-known way to ease into rest. The wind-down starts at a gentle pace and stretches as you relax.
