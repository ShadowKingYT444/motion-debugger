How it works

One night, in four quiet movements.

Scroll through a single illustrative night. Drift reads you, then the room, then answers: softly, and only when it helps.

10:30 pm Awake
  1. 01

    Wind down

    From the moment you open Drift, the app eases the evening off. The lights suggestion dims, a 4-7-8 breathing guide starts, and a slow tide fades in underneath.

  2. 02

    Settle

    Phone-on-bed motion and breathing sounds show when you go still. The tide recedes and a soft brown-noise bed takes over, masking the room without calling attention to itself.

  3. 03

    Hold the deep

    In deep sleep Drift does almost nothing. A steady pink-noise cushion holds the room still, and any change is slow enough that you will never notice it.

  4. 04

    Wake without a jolt

    Inside your wake window Drift waits for a light-sleep moment, then lifts the mix with warm tones and a slow sunrise. You surface instead of being dragged up.

The science

Sleep is not a switch. It is four states, cycling roughly every ninety minutes, and each one needs something different from the room around it. Drift treats them differently.

Quiet the mind

Falling asleep is mostly about lowering arousal. A slow, masking tide and a paced breath give a busy mind something gentle to follow instead of a ceiling to stare at.

What Drift does

Fades in a slow tide, paces your breath at 4-7-8 and keeps the volume curve below the noise of the room.
