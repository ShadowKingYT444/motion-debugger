The idea

Most speakers point at you. Halo surrounds you. One ring of six tweeters, a single woofer, and a room that quietly stops feeling like a room.

The object

Four parts. No seams.

01

The halo

One ring of light, set into a machined cap. It breathes with the music, and answers touch: tap to pause, turn your fingertip around it to set the volume.

02

The sleeve

A seamless circular-knit fabric, woven for sound before looks. It is acoustically transparent, and the cover lifts off for washing or a new colour.

03

The core

Six 25 mm silk-dome tweeters in a ring, a 130 mm long-throw woofer firing down, two passive radiators, and seven amplifiers: one for every driver.

04

The foundation

A cast aluminium base ring on a smoked oak plinth. 1.1 kg of mass at the bottom keeps the whole cylinder still when the low notes arrive.

The sound technology

One point. Every corner.

Six tweeters sit in a ring, phase-matched so the sound leaves in every direction at once. A woofer below fills the floor. Halo listens to the room with its own sweep on first boot, then corrects for walls, shelves and sofas.

Materials

Made to be touched, then kept.

Five materials, each chosen for how it sounds and how it ages.

01

Circular-knit sleeve

One seamless tube of recycled polyester and wool, knitted to the cylinder's exact size. Three colours; the cover is replaceable.

  • 38% recycled
  • Washable
02

Anodised aluminium

The cap and base ring are turned from a single bar, brushed by hand, then anodised in a pale champagne that will not chip or fade.

  • 100% recycled alloy
  • Hand finished
03

A ring of light

A diffused light guide, 2 mm wide, runs around the cap. It is the only interface on the object, and it dims itself after dark.

  • Touch sensing
  • Warm 2200 K
04

Smoked oak plinth

FSC-certified oak, smoked rather than stained, so the grain stays crisp. It decouples the speaker from the shelf below.

  • FSC oak
  • Oiled
05

10 yrs of spare parts. Every screw is a standard one.

Halo opens with a hex key. Batteries, covers and drivers are sold as parts, with repair guides published at launch.

  • Repairable
  • Takeback programme
