Made to be touched.

Three materials and nothing else, each chosen for how it sounds and how it ages. If a part does not earn its place, it is not on the speaker.

Undyed wool-blend knit with champagne anodised aluminium.

Knitted in Herning

One seamless tube of wool and recycled polyester, knitted to shape on Danish looms. About 26,000 stitches and acoustically transparent, so the drivers behind it never feel veiled.

Turned, not stamped

Crown and base are turned from 100% recycled aluminium and anodised in-house. Each is finished by hand and laser-etched with the serial number of its speaker.

2700 kelvin, no more

The ring only ever shows warm light. No colour cycling, no animation for its own sake: it breathes with the music and sleeps with the room.

Five things it does so you don’t have to.

  1. 1

    Listens before it plays

    A six-second sweep and six microphones map your room. Adaptive Room Tuning rebuilds the filter set around the walls, the rug and the shelf behind it. Repeat it whenever you move the furniture.

  2. 2

    Pairs in a breath

    Place two Halos in a room, hold both crowns for a second and they become a true stereo pair, with channels assigned by the microphones. No cables, no app required.

  3. 3

    Follows you from room to room

    Hold the crown and the music moves to the nearest Halo, in sync to within a tenth of a millisecond. Link up to twelve speakers across the house.

  4. 4

    Knows when to whisper

    Night mode narrows dynamic range and rolls off the low end below what the neighbours can feel, while the ring dims to a single warm thread of light.

  5. 5

    Plays everything, losslessly

    AirPlay 2, Spotify Connect, Tidal Connect, Roon and Bluetooth LE Audio, up to 24-bit / 192 kHz over Wi-Fi 6 or Ethernet. Or plug in with USB-C.
