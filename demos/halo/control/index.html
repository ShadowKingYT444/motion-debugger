The idea

Most speakers aim sound at you. Halo lets it go in every direction at once, so music arrives the way light does in a good room — from everywhere, and from nowhere in particular.

The object

Five parts.
One quiet object.

  1. A single block of 62 % recycled aluminium, turned in Aarhus and finished by hand. The ring on top is the only control you need: touch, turn, rest.

  2. A seamless wool-blend knit, woven to the exact curve of the cylinder. Acoustically transparent, and it comes off to be washed.

  3. Six silk-dome tweeters and four midrange drivers sit in a single ring, each aimed outward at a precise offset so their output sums to a circle.

  4. A six-inch long-throw woofer in a sealed 3.2 L chamber, flanked by two passive radiators that cancel each other’s vibration. Deep, and perfectly still on the shelf.

  5. A conical diffuser throws the bass across the floor. Beneath it, a warm ring of light — the halo — shows what Halo is doing, and nothing else.

A — Aura array

Ten drivers, one circle.

Six 19 mm silk-dome tweeters and four 2-inch midrange drivers share a ring. Each pair is phase-aligned in software so neighbouring drivers add up to a smooth wavefront instead of a comb of peaks and nulls.

B — RoomSense

It listens before it plays.

Four MEMS microphones send a sweep through the room and read back every wall, sofa and window. A 40-second measurement sets a filter that removes bloom and slap-back, then runs quietly in the background.

C — Low chamber

Bass you feel in the floor.

A down-firing woofer drives a conical diffuser, so low frequencies spread across the floor rather than pointing at a wall. Two opposed passive radiators keep the cabinet still, even at full volume.

Materials

Made to be
lived beside.

Three finishes, each dyed, anodised and knitted to match. Choose one — the whole page follows.

  • Wool-blend knit

    Woven seamless in one piece, 58 % recycled yarn. Zips off the chassis; machine-washable at 30 °C.

  • Anodised aluminium

    Turned from a single billet, 62 % recycled, bead-blasted by hand and anodised in a colour to match the knit.

  • Frosted light guide

    The halo is a ring of 96 LEDs behind a milled acrylic diffuser. It is warm, never white, and never brighter than a candle.

  • Natural rubber feet

    A plinth of damped natural rubber isolates Halo from the surface it sits on, and will not mark oak, marble or glass.
