The problem with sleep sounds

Most sleep apps play the same rain every night. But your nights are never the same. Tonight you are wired from a late meeting. Tomorrow you are simply tired. Drift notices the difference, and answers it softly.

How it works

Three quiet things happen while you drift.

  1. 01

    Wind down

    An hour before your bedtime, Drift reads the evening you had (screen time, movement, how late you ate) and builds a routine that fits: breathing, dimmer light, a calmer sound.

  2. 02

    Adapt

    Through the night it listens for restlessness through your phone or wearable, and softens, thins or deepens the soundscape in small steps you never notice.

  3. 03

    Wake gently

    Drift looks for your lightest stretch of sleep inside the window you set, and brings the morning in slowly, with sound first, then light.

The science

Built on what sleep research already agrees on.

Sleep cycles

90min

A typical sleep cycle lasts about ninety minutes. Drift times your alarm to land between cycles, not inside deep sleep.

Slow breathing

6breaths / min

Slow, paced breathing near six breaths a minute is associated with a calmer nervous system. The wind-down paces you there.

Steady sound

Pinknoise

Steady, even sound masks sudden noises that cause micro-awakenings. Drift shapes its level and tone over the night rather than looping one track.

Cool room

18°C · about 65°F

Most guidance puts the ideal bedroom between roughly 15 and 19 degrees. Drift reminds you to cool the room as the routine begins.

A note on claims

Drift is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders. If you snore loudly, stop breathing at night or feel unwell in the day, talk to a clinician.
