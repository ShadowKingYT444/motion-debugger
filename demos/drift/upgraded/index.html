A night isn’t one long block. It’s four or five ninety-minute cycles of light, deep and REM sleep. Drift works with that architecture instead of against it.

Circadian timing

Light and temperature tell your brain when night begins. Drift schedules your wind-down to your own chronotype, not a one-size-fits-all bedtime.

Sleep architecture

Waking from light sleep feels easier than breaking into deep sleep. The smart window looks for the lightest moment near your alarm.

Sound that masks and settles

Steady, broadband sound can mask sudden noises that cause micro-arousals. Drift changes the mix with your sleep stage rather than looping one track.

Slow breathing

Paced breathing before bed can lower heart rate and ease the shift out of alertness. Drift paces it with a light you can follow with eyes half closed.
