The science

Sleep isn't one thing, it's a conversation between your body clock, your nervous system and your surroundings. Drift doesn't force you to sleep. It listens, then removes what's in the way.

0min

Faster to fall asleep

Median improvement after four weeks, as breath-paced soundscapes lower pre-sleep arousal.*

0%

More deep sleep

Gentle, stage-aware masking keeps small noises from fragmenting slow-wave sleep.*

0/5

Morning energy

Waking from lighter sleep instead of mid-cycle makes the first hour feel different.*

Circadian alignment

Your body clock decides when sleep pressure feels natural. Drift times light cues, wind-down and wake windows to your rhythm, not a generic 11 pm.

Acoustic pacing

Steady, slowly-varying sound can mask disruptions and give the mind something gentle to follow. Drift gradually slows tempo toward restful breathing.

Pre-sleep arousal

A racing mind is the most common reason we stay awake. A short guided wind-down helps the nervous system step down before the lights go out.

*Illustrative figures from a 2,400-person internal pilot. Drift is a wellness product, not a medical device, and does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders.
