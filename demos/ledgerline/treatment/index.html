The product

One call. Four guarantees. Every line balanced.

  1. 01

    Request

    Send one idempotent call. Retries are safe by construction.

  2. 02

    Authorize

    Balances, limits and screening are checked and a hold is placed in 12 ms.

  3. 03

    Post

    Double-entry lines are written atomically. Debits must equal credits or nothing posts.

  4. 04

    Settle

    The rail confirms, balances move and a signed webhook tells your system.
